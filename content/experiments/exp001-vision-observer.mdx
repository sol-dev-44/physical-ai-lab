## Why

In Experiment 0 a person watches every trial and types y or n. That doesn't scale, and it
can't run while nobody is watching. If a cheap camera can judge as well as a person, every
later experiment gets automatic evaluation, and the reinforcement learning experiment gets
its reward signal.

## Setup

A taped rectangle on the floor, a webcam looking straight down, and two stickers on the
toy robot: green on its head, pink on its back. The lab tracks both stickers, works out
where the robot is and which way it faces, and decides after each command whether it
moved the way the command says.

The camera also guards the robot. No fresh sighting, no motion, and a move that would end
near the tape is refused before it's sent.

## Protocol

1. Calibrate the arena from one frame with nobody in it.
2. Ten trials each of forward and reverse, judged by the camera and by a person, who
   answers before seeing the camera's verdict.
3. A null test: the same trials with nothing sent.
4. Report agreement, both kinds of disagreement, and the null-test score.

<Callout>Frames are processed and dropped. Only numbers are kept, and no image from this experiment is published.</Callout>
