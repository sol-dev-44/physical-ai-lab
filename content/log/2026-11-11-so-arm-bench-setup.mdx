This is the first bench session with the SO-ARM101 Pro, a LeRobot arm kit with a
black leader arm and a white follower. No experiment run, no camera, nothing synced.
The full bench notes are in `experiments/exp002-so-arm-bench/README.md`.

## What worked

- Every motor answered on IDs 1 to 6 with no ID changes needed.
- The follower calibrated and saved all six joints.
- Teleop ran at 60 Hz with a speed cap. It worked in a rough sense, and the calibration still needs tuning.

## What went wrong

- **Pressing Enter at the calibration prompt reuses the old file.** The prompt offers
  "Enter" to reuse or `c` to recalibrate. Enter alone rewrote the old file to the
  motors and recorded nothing new. Type `c` first.
- **A calibration run can only widen its min and max.** Once a joint was pushed past its
  normal range, that extreme stayed in the saved file. Ctrl-C cancels a run safely, but
  it can't undo values already recorded.
- **Full-turn ranges are a warning sign.** Two leader joints were saved across nearly the
  whole 0 to 4095 turn, which means the reading wrapped or the joint went past its normal bend.
- **The leader's joints went quiet.** After one recalibration, the elbow, wrist flex and
  gripper didn't change while the arm was moved by hand, though shoulder pan and wrist roll did.
  Torque was off and the load was zero, so the motors weren't resisting. The next calibration
  fixed the gripper. The cause isn't confirmed yet.
- **One dropout on the follower.** Teleop failed to enable torque on the wrist roll,
  before anything moved. A read-only check found every motor fine afterwards, and a clean
  reconnect got teleop running.

## Safety decision

Teleop ran with a per-update speed cap (`max_relative_target=5`) because the leader's
ranges weren't yet trustworthy. The cap is a deliberate change from the vendor command.
Both arms were clamped to the bench, with the follower's power switch within reach.

## Next session

Recalibrate the leader while keeping each joint in its normal bend, check the follower's
wrist roll, then run teleop again before removing the cap. The arm isn't in the lab harness
yet: integration waits until the calibration is solid.
